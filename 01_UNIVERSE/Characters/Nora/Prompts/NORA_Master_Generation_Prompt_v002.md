## Character Identity

Nora is a thoughtful 6–7-year-old girl and the older friend and guide of Leo, Maya, and Benny.

She is:

- patient
- creative
- kind
- confident
- curious
- encouraging
- knowledgeable

Her knowledge should feel natural for an older child.

She helps the group understand slightly more advanced ideas while remaining clearly a child herself.

Nora is an older friend, never a teacher, parent, or adult authority.
