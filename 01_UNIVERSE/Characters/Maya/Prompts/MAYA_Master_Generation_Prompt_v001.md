# Maya — Master Generation Prompt v001

## Purpose

Canonical generation specification for maintaining Maya consistently across image and video generation.

## Character Reference

**Reference ID:** MAYA-REF-001  
**Design:** Concept 04 — The Thinker  
**Status:** CANONICAL / LOCKED

The canonical Maya reference images are the primary visual source of truth.

## Character Identity

Maya is a cheerful 4–5-year-old preschool girl.

She is intelligent, creative, thoughtful, curious, confident, kind, supportive, observant, encouraging, and playful.

Her intelligence is natural and age-appropriate. She should never appear or behave like an adult.

## Locked Visual DNA

- Warm medium-brown skin
- Large warm-brown expressive eyes
- Rounded preschool facial proportions
- Dark-brown hair
- Two rounded hair buns
- Purple headband
- One small yellow five-point star hair clip on Maya's anatomical LEFT side
- Lavender short-sleeve top
- Warm yellow lightbulb emblem centered on the front of the top
- Purple skirt with subtle light-lavender polka dots
- White sneakers with purple accents
- Rounded premium preschool 3D body proportions

## Absolute Consistency Rules

1. Preserve Maya's exact facial identity.
2. Preserve her facial proportions.
3. Preserve her warm medium-brown skin.
4. Preserve her warm-brown eyes.
5. Preserve the two-bun hairstyle.
6. Preserve bun size, placement, and silhouette.
7. Preserve the purple headband.
8. Preserve the yellow star hair clip.
9. The star clip must remain on Maya's anatomical LEFT side.
10. Never mirror Maya's character design.
11. Never duplicate the star clip.
12. Preserve the lavender top.
13. Preserve the yellow lightbulb emblem.
14. Preserve the purple skirt and polka-dot pattern.
15. Preserve the white-and-purple sneakers.
16. Preserve Maya's preschool body proportions.
17. Do not age Maya.
18. Do not introduce random accessories.
19. Do not redesign Maya's hairstyle.
20. Do not silently modify any locked visual feature.

## Camera Reference

The canonical camera rotation system is:

- 0° — Front
- 45° — Front-left
- 90° — Left profile
- 135° — Back-left
- 180° — Back
- 225° — Back-right
- 270° — Right profile
- 315° — Front-right

The angle describes the camera position around Maya.

Maya remains the same physical character while the camera moves around her.

Maya's anatomical left and right must never be interpreted as viewer-left and viewer-right.

## Scene Variation

The following may change according to the episode:

- pose
- action
- facial expression
- environment
- props
- camera movement
- framing
- lighting
- time of day

These changes must never alter Maya's core visual identity.

## Signature Notebook

Maya's purple idea/story notebook is a scene-dependent prop.

It is NOT a permanent part of her physical character design.

Use it only when required by the story.

## Quality Control

Reject a generation if:

- Maya's face changes substantially
- hairstyle changes
- either hair bun changes substantially
- headband disappears or changes
- star clip moves to the anatomical right side
- duplicate star clips appear
- clothing colors change
- lightbulb emblem disappears or changes substantially
- skirt pattern changes substantially
- shoes change substantially
- Maya appears older than 4–5
- body proportions change substantially
- random accessories appear
- Maya no longer matches MAYA-REF-001
