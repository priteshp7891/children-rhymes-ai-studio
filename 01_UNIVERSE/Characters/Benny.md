# Benny — Character Bible

**Character ID:** CHAR-03
**Reference ID:** BENNY-REF-001
**Version:** v001
**Status:** CANONICAL / LOCKED

## Role

Puppy / comedy and emotional companion

## Age / Character Scale

Young puppy with small, preschool-friendly proportions.

Benny should clearly read as a young puppy, never as an adult dog.

## Personality

- Playful
- Affectionate
- Curious
- Mischievous
- Easily excited
- Friendly
- Energetic
- Emotionally expressive

Benny is naturally curious and often gets excited by simple things.

He can accidentally create funny situations through his curiosity and enthusiasm. His comedy should feel innocent and age-appropriate.

Benny is affectionate toward Leo, Maya, and the other children and provides emotional warmth when a scene requires it.

## Story Function

Benny creates funny or unexpected situations and provides visual comedy.

He can:

- React dramatically to interesting things
- Follow the children around
- Investigate objects
- Get excited easily
- Make playful mistakes
- Express curiosity through body language
- Provide emotional reactions
- Create lighthearted moments

Benny communicates heavily through facial expressions, ears, tail movement, body posture, and playful actions.

1. Benny must remain the same physical character in every generation.
2. Facial identity must remain consistent.
3. Eye shape and eye color must remain consistent.
4. Muzzle proportions must remain consistent.
5. Warm golden-brown fur must remain consistent.
6. Cream facial markings must remain consistent.
7. Cream chest and paw markings must remain consistent.
8. Floppy ear shape and proportions must remain consistent.
9. Body proportions must remain consistent.
10. Tail shape and overall silhouette must remain consistent.
11. The deep-blue collar must remain present.
12. Exactly one yellow five-point star tag must be present.
13. The star tag must remain small and proportionate to the collar.
14. The star tag must never be duplicated.
15. Random accessories must not be introduced.
16. Clothing must not be introduced unless explicitly approved for a specific production requirement.
17. Benny must remain a young puppy.
18. Benny must not be redesigned as a different dog breed or radically different puppy.
19. Benny must remain visually compatible with Leo, Maya, and the Children Rhymes AI universe.
20. Camera angle, pose, expression, environment, lighting, and scene-specific action may change.
21. Benny's core visual identity may not change without an explicit approved design revision.
