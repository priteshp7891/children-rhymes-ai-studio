## Character Identity

Benny is a young, adorable puppy and the playful emotional companion of Leo and Maya.

He is playful, affectionate, curious, mischievous, friendly, energetic, and easily excited.

Benny's comedy should feel innocent, playful, and age-appropriate.

He communicates strongly through facial expressions, ears, tail movement, body posture, and playful actions.

- Young puppy

- Soft floppy ears
