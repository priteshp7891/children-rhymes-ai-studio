# Nora — Character Bible

**Character ID:** CHAR-05
**Reference ID:** NORA-REF-001
**Version:** v001
**Status:** CANONICAL / LOCKED

## Role

Older friend / guide

## Age / Character Scale

6–7 years old

Nora is an older friend who has slightly more experience than Leo and Maya, but she is still a child. She should never appear or behave like a teacher, parent, or adult authority.

## Personality

- Patient
- Thoughtful
- Encouraging
- Creative
- Kind
- Confident
- Curious
- Knowledgeable
- Helpful
- Capable of making mistakes too

Nora enjoys drawing, creating things, exploring, and helping her friends understand new ideas.

Her knowledge should feel natural for an older child. She can guide the younger characters without behaving like an adult.

## Story Function

Nora helps the group understand slightly more advanced concepts.

She can:

- Offer helpful ideas
- Guide the younger children
- Explain simple concepts
- Encourage others
- Notice details
- Help organize an activity
- Make mistakes and learn from them
- Support Leo, Maya, and Benny

Nora is an older friend, not a teacher or parent.
