# Children Rhymes AI

## Master Universe Bible & Production Constitution

> **Purpose:** Single source of truth for the children's YouTube  
> channel, its creative universe, production standards, automation  
> architecture, publishing system, and long-term IP strategy.

---

# 1. Project Identity

**Project:** Children Rhymes AI Video Generator  
**Market:** United States  
**Primary audience:** Preschool children ages 2--5  
**Secondary audience:** Parents, caregivers, and preschool educators  
**Language:** American English  
**Initial publishing target:** 4 videos/week  
**Long-term target:** 5--7 videos/week, including compilations when  
justified

## Core mission

Build an original preschool entertainment and educational media brand  
powered heavily by AI, while maintaining human creative direction,  
quality control, originality, and a recognizable children's IP universe.

## Strategic principle

> **We are building a children's media studio, not an AI content farm.**

AI is used to increase production speed, experimentation, consistency,  
and scalability. AI does not replace creative judgment, educational  
responsibility, quality control, or final publishing decisions.

---

# 2. Production Constitution

## 2.1 Audience

The core audience is U.S. preschoolers approximately 2--5 years old.

Content should feel naturally understandable to an American preschool  
audience.

Use:

- American English vocabulary
    
- natural American pronunciation
    
- familiar preschool routines
    
- age-appropriate humor
    
- diverse characters and environments
    
- U.S.-relevant seasonal opportunities where appropriate
    

Avoid forcing cultural references simply to appear American.

---

# 3. Brand Positioning

The channel sits between:

**preschool education + musical entertainment**

The intended experience is:

> "This is fun to watch and sing along with, and I happen to be learning  
> something."

The channel should not feel like a classroom disguised as entertainment.

# 8. Episode Structure

Default musical structure:

1. **0:00--0:05** --- visual/music hook
    
2. **0:05--0:20** --- introduce subject
    
3. **0:20--0:50** --- main hook
    
4. **0:50--1:30** --- first verse/action
    
5. **1:30--2:00** --- variation/participation
    
6. **2:00--2:40** --- repetition
    
7. **2:40--3:10** --- final variation/conclusion
    

Exact structure may change based on the song.

---

# 9. Hook Rules

The opening seconds must quickly communicate:

- character
    
- action
    
- subject
    
- curiosity
    

Avoid:

- long logo sequences
    
- slow intros
    
- empty establishing shots
    
- unnecessary narration
    
- generic title cards
    

The content begins quickly.

---

# 10. Music Constitution

Music is a primary product asset.

Every original song should prioritize:

- simple melody
    
- memorable hook
    
- repetition
    
- clear rhythm
    
- child-friendly vocal range
    
- understandable lyrics
    
- physical actions where appropriate
    
- predictable structure
    
- short phrases
    
- call-and-response opportunities
    

The song should remain understandable without relying entirely on the  
visuals.

---

# 11. Lyric Rules

Lyrics must be:

- age appropriate
    
- grammatically sound
    
- easy to pronounce
    
- easy to remember
    
- visually representable
    
- meaningful
    

Avoid:

- complicated vocabulary
    
- confusing metaphors
    
- frightening imagery
    
- adult humor
    
- double meanings
    
- unsafe instructions
    
- meaningless AI-generated word sequences
    

Every educational song needs a clear learning objective.

# 19. Video Generation Constitution

AI video is generated as controlled scenes.

Default scene duration:

**approximately 4--8 seconds**

Each scene requires:

- Scene ID
    
- lyrics/narration
    
- visual description
    
- characters
    
- action
    
- environment
    
- camera
    
- duration
    
- audio requirement
    
- generation prompt
    
- QC status
    

The final video is assembled from approved scenes.

---

# 20. Audio/Visual Synchronization

Visual action must directly reinforce the lyrics.

Example:

If the lyric says:

> "Jump, jump, jump!"

the characters should visibly jump.

If the lyric says:

> "Find the red ball!"

the red ball must be visually obvious and relevant.

The visuals should teach/reinforce the words rather than merely decorate  
them.

---

# 21. Voice Constitution

Recurring characters use consistent voice identities.

Each voice record must contain:

- provider
    
- plan
    
- model
    
- voice ID
    
- generation date
    
- commercial-use status
    

Do not imitate celebrities or recognizable public figures.

---

# 22. Audio Rights Ledger

Every generated audio asset must record:

- Asset ID
    
- provider
    
- plan/account type
    
- generation date
    
- model
    
- prompt
    
- lyrics
    
- license status
    
- commercial-use status
    
- final approval
    

No asset should enter publishing without a known rights status.

---

# 23. Copyright Constitution

Do not use copyrighted third-party material without appropriate rights.

Traditional nursery rhymes require individual rights verification  
because the underlying work, lyrics, arrangement, adaptation, and  
recording may have different rights statuses.

Whenever appropriate, create original:

- recordings
    
- arrangements
    
- visuals
    
- adaptations
    
- compositions

One long-form song can produce multiple Shorts.

- Can You Find Red?

- Find the Blue Ball!

Shorts should support the main content library.

# 30. Thumbnail Constitution

Default thumbnail structure:

- one primary character
    
- one clear object/concept
    
- strong expression
    
- simple background
    
- minimal text
    

Preferred text:

**1--4 words**

Examples:

- LEARN COLORS!
    
- ABC FUN!
    
- DINOSAUR DANCE!
    
- FIND RED!
    
- COUNT WITH ME!
    

Avoid clutter.

# 31. Title Constitution

Titles prioritize:

1. clarity
    
2. parent understanding
    
3. natural search language
    
4. topic
    
5. recognizable format
    

Examples:

- Learn Colors with Leo | Red, Blue, Yellow & Green
    
- Five Little Ducks | Nursery Rhyme for Kids
    
- ABC Phonics Song | Learn Letter Sounds
    

No deceptive titles or keyword stuffing.

# 32. Made-for-Kids Principle

This is a preschool channel and should be operated according to the  
actual YouTube made-for-kids requirements.

We do not attempt to circumvent:

- audience designation
    
- advertising restrictions
    
- feature restrictions
    
- children's content quality requirements
    

The business model must account for reduced engagement features and lack  
of personalized advertising on made-for-kids content.

# 34. Anti-Content-Farm Rule

Do not create hundreds of nearly identical videos by changing only one  
noun.

Example of prohibited pattern:

- Red Song
    
- Blue Song
    
- Green Song
    
- Yellow Song
    

with identical melody, animation, structure, and visuals.

Variation must be meaningful.

---

# 35. Educational Integrity

If a video teaches something, the information must be correct.

Examples:

- phonics must be accurate
    
- counting must be accurate
    
- colors must be represented correctly
    
- animal facts must be correct
    
- science concepts must be accurate
    

AI output is not considered authoritative merely because an AI generated  
it.

---

# 36. Safety Constitution

Avoid:

- dangerous challenges
    
- realistic injury
    
- disturbing transformations
    
- frightening imagery
    
- inappropriate medical content
    
- unsafe behavior presented as acceptable
    
- weapons
    
- adult themes
    
- sexual content
    
- graphic violence
    

Children's entertainment remains age appropriate.
