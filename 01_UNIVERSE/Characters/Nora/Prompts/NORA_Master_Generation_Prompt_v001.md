# Nora — Master Generation Prompt v001

## Purpose

Canonical generation specification for maintaining Nora consistently across image and video generation.

## Character Reference

**Reference ID:** NORA-REF-001
**Design:** Concept 02 — Creative Guide
**Status:** CANONICAL / LOCKED

The canonical Nora reference images are the primary visual source of truth.

## Character Identity

Nora is a thoughtful 6–7-year-old girl and the older friend and guide of Leo, Maya, and Benny.

She is patient, creative, kind, confident, curious, encouraging, and knowledgeable.

Her knowledge should feel natural for an older child. She helps the group understand slightly more advanced ideas while remaining clearly a child herself.

Nora is an older friend, never a teacher, parent, or adult authority.

## Locked Visual DNA

- Warm medium-brown skin
- Large expressive warm-brown eyes
- Soft rounded child-friendly face
- Long wavy dark-brown hair
- Teal headband
- One coral bow attached to the headband
- Coral tunic
- Teal leggings
- Orange-and-cream sneakers
- Tan crossbody art bag
- Premium preschool 3D animation style
- Rounded child-friendly proportions

## Locked Color Identity

- Primary: Coral
- Secondary: Teal
- Accent: Tan
- Footwear accent: Orange
- Neutral: Cream

Nora must remain visually distinct from Maya.

Do not use lavender or purple as Nora's dominant clothing color.

## Clothing Rules

Nora has NO emblem or symbol on her clothing.

Do not add:

- Star
- Lightbulb
- Compass
- Badge
- Logo
- Decorative chest symbol

Her identity comes from her color palette, hairstyle, headband, bow, silhouette, and art bag.

## Signature Accessory

Nora's tan crossbody art bag is part of her canonical character design.

The bag should remain visually consistent.

Art supplies may appear when required by the scene, but individual contents are not permanently locked.

## Absolute Consistency Rules

1. Preserve Nora's exact facial identity.
2. Preserve facial proportions.
3. Preserve warm medium-brown skin.
4. Preserve warm-brown eyes.
5. Preserve long wavy dark-brown hair.
6. Preserve hair length, volume, and silhouette.
7. Preserve the teal headband.
8. Preserve the coral bow and its position.
9. Preserve the coral tunic.
10. Preserve the teal leggings.
11. Preserve the tan crossbody art bag.
12. Preserve the orange-and-cream sneakers.
13. Do not add an emblem to Nora's clothing.
14. Do not add a star, lightbulb, compass, badge, or logo.
15. Do not introduce random accessories.
16. Do not substantially alter Nora's body proportions.
17. Do not age Nora into an adult.
18. Do not make Nora resemble Maya.
19. Preserve Nora's older-friend identity.
20. Nora must remain visually compatible with Leo, Maya, Benny, and the Children Rhymes AI universe.
21. Camera angle, pose, expression, environment, lighting, and scene-specific action may change.
22. Nora's core visual identity may not change without explicit approval.

## Camera Reference

The canonical camera rotation system is:

- 0° — Front
- 45° — Front-left
- 90° — Left profile
- 135° — Back-left
- 180° — Back
- 225° — Back-right
- 270° — Right profile
- 315° — Front-right

The angle describes the camera position around Nora.

Nora remains the same physical character while the camera moves around her.

## Scene Variation

The following may change according to the episode:

- pose
- action
- facial expression
- environment
- scene-specific props
- camera movement
- framing
- lighting
- time of day

These changes must never alter Nora's core visual identity.

## Quality Control

Reject a generation if:

- Nora's face changes substantially
- facial proportions change substantially
- hair changes substantially
- headband disappears or changes
- bow disappears or changes substantially
- clothing colors change
- lavender or purple becomes the dominant clothing color
- an emblem appears on the clothing
- a star appears on the clothing
- a lightbulb appears on the clothing
- a compass appears on the clothing
- a badge or logo appears on the clothing
- the art bag disappears or changes substantially
- shoes change substantially
- Nora appears substantially older
- body proportions change substantially
- random accessories appear
- Nora no longer matches NORA-REF-001
