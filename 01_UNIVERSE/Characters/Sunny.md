## Role

Bird / discovery companion

## Age / Character Scale

Not separately documented.

Sunny should retain the scale and proportions established by the canonical Concept 03 character sheet and approved production references.

## Personality

- Energetic
- Observant
- Adventurous
- Easily excited

Sunny is naturally curious about the world around them. They notice details that others may miss and become excited when something interesting is discovered.

## Story Function

Sunny enables discovery, searches, and environmental reveals.

They can:

- Notice things the others have not seen
- Draw attention toward interesting details
- Help the group discover hidden or unexpected things
- Participate in searches
- Explore environments
- React with excitement to discoveries
- Help reveal information through observation

Sunny is a companion and discovery character. They should not behave like a teacher, parent, or adult authority.
