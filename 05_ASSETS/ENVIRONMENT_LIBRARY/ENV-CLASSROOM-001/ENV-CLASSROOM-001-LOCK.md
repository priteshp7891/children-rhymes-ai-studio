## 1. Purpose

`ENV-CLASSROOM-001` is the canonical preschool classroom environment for Little Zipper House.

It is one fixed physical space viewed from different camera positions. Different views must never become different classroom designs.

---

## 2. Canonical Reference

The canonical visual source is:

`ENV-CLASSROOM-001-Canonical-Reference-Sheet.png`

The reference sheet contains coordinated views of the same physical classroom.

## 4. Spatial Continuity

The following must remain fixed across views and shots:

- room geometry and proportions
- wall positions
- ceiling height
- door position and proportions
- window positions and proportions
- whiteboard
- number-learning display
- storage units
- shelves
- reading area
- art area
- block/play area
- central rug
- round activity table
- chairs
- plants
- major decorative elements
- floor treatment
- wall treatment
- ceiling treatment

Camera position may change. The physical environment may not.

---

## 5. Fixed-Object Rule

Major environmental objects are spatially anchored.

They must not move, disappear, duplicate, resize, redesign, recolor, or change material between views unless formally revised and re-locked.

Small story props may be repositioned when explicitly required by the shot.

---

## 6. Camera Freedom

The following may change:

- camera position
- camera angle
- camera height
- camera distance
- framing
- lens/composition
- camera movement
- character position
- character action

These changes must not alter the underlying classroom.

---

## 7. Environment Identity

The classroom must retain the same:

- preschool visual identity
- architectural structure
- furniture language
- material language
- color palette
- lighting character
- scale relationships
- decorative language

Never generate a generic replacement classroom.

---

## 8. Character Separation

Characters are independent canonical assets.

Production composition follows:

`ENVIRONMENT INGREDIENT + CANONICAL CHARACTER INGREDIENT(S) + SHOT ACTION`

The classroom reference must not redefine character appearance.

---

## 9. Character Scale

The canonical scale reference establishes believable relationships between characters and classroom architecture/furniture.

Locked character relationship:

- Nora is slightly taller than Leo and Maya.
- Leo and Maya are approximately equal in height.

Character position may change without changing environmental scale.

---

## 10. Lighting Continuity

Preserve the lighting direction and general lighting character established by the canonical reference.

Lighting may change only when an approved production specification explicitly requires a story or time-of-day change.

---

## 11. Materials and Colors

Major materials, finishes, and color relationships shown in the canonical reference are part of the environment identity.

Do not substitute unrelated wall, floor, furniture, or decorative treatments between shots.

---

## 12. Forbidden Changes

Unless the environment is formally revised:

- no classroom redesign
- no room-dimension changes
- no door/window relocation
- no window-count changes
- no major furniture relocation
- no major furniture additions/removals
- no architectural-style changes
- no major material changes
- no major palette changes
- no generic classroom replacement
- no invented geometry that contradicts the reference views

---

## 13. Google Flow Reference Guidance

When environment references/Ingredients are supported:

1. Use `ENV-CLASSROOM-001` as the environment reference.
2. Use the canonical reference sheet as the primary environment source.
3. Resolve the required camera view from the reference sheet.
4. Add canonical character references separately.
5. Specify the required shot action and camera movement.
6. Do not redesign the classroom in the shot prompt.
7. Do not allow the model to invent a different classroom to satisfy the prompt.

The environment reference defines **where** the action happens.

The character reference defines **who** is present.

The shot specification defines **what happens and how the camera behaves**.

`ENV-CLASSROOM-001`

---

## 15. Continuity QC

A generated shot fails environment QC if a major environmental element:

- moves unexpectedly
- changes scale
- changes design
- changes material
- changes color
- disappears without justification
- appears in an impossible spatial position
- contradicts another canonical view

Visual attractiveness does not override continuity.

---

## 16. Revision Policy

This environment is locked.

Any change to geometry, furniture layout, architecture, materials, major colors, lighting identity, or canonical views requires a new environment revision.

The current locked version must not be silently overwritten.

Future revisions use versioned identifiers such as:

`ENV-CLASSROOM-001-V002`
